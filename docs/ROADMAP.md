## Game concept (short)

**Lore:** The Watchers guard the firmament. A rift in the sky rains star fragments upon the world, and the
*Eclipse* – a fallen fifth Watcher – awakens. The player rises from seeker to aspirant.

| Watcher | Domain | Rewards | Gear line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solenne | Sun | Fighting by day, slaying undead | Battle axe *Dawnbreaker*, sword *Halo Edge* |
| Vael | Moon | Night hunts, precision hits | Bow *Crescent*, crossbow *Tidecaller* |
| Orrin | Starsmith | Ores, building constellations | Armour *Seraph Plate*, starlight shield |
| Ilyra | Chronicler | Ruins, lore fragments, scans | Codex, rituals, automation |

Favour is partly opposed – nobody can max all four.

Core systems: Celestial Alignment & Astrolabe · Starfall · Constellations (Star Anchors) · Starlight & Beams ·
Convergence Altar · Eclipse Gaze · Living Gear (3 sigil slots, stances) · Fallen Observatories (24 lore fragments) ·
Firmament dimension with four sanctum trials and a three-phase Eclipse boss.
