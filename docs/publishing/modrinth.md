# Astral Vigil

*The Watchers guard the firmament. A rift in the sky rains star fragments upon the world – and the Eclipse awakens.*

**Astral Vigil** is a progression mod about starlight. Catch falling stars, read the sky, guide beams of light across
your base, forge celestial metals in convergence rituals and wield gear that awakens through your deeds – then rise
into the **Firmament**, earn the favour of four Watchers and face their fallen sibling, **the Eclipse**.

## Features

- **Starfall** – fragments of the sky crash down on clear nights, leaving craters and Fallen Stars to mine.
- **The night sky matters** – starlight depends on the time, the moon phase and the weather; the **Astrolabe** reads it.
- **Four alignments** – research *Sol*, *Luna*, *Stella* and *Void* blocks and solve constellations on the Star Chart.
- **Starlight networks** – Collectors, Lenses, Mirrors, Prisms and Reservoirs; visible beams, push-based and cheap
  on the server.
- **Convergence rituals** – pedestals, an altar, an Astral Forge. Symmetry and aligned blocks keep a ritual stable;
  instability flings offerings and calls creatures.
- **Living gear** – six pieces of Sunsteel, Moonsilver and Starmetal gear that awaken through deeds, take Sigils and
  unleash abilities. Top tier is on par with Netherite – never above.
- **Fallen Observatories** – ruins with Constellation Locks, 24 pages of lore and Star Anchor rituals.
- **Eclipse Gaze** – heavy use of celestial power draws attention… whispers, Echoes, void crystals.
- **The Firmament** – a floating sky dimension with low gravity and four sanctums with trials: a climb, an archery
  hunt, a puzzle of light and a library maze.
- **The Eclipse** – a three-phase boss with telegraphed attacks, Echo waves and Umbral Novas. Rewards: the Halo of
  Dawn and toggleable **Seraph Wings**.
- **Codex Caelestis** – an in-game guide (with Patchouli) that grows with your research.
- **Fully translated** – English and German.

## Integrations

JEI · EMI · Jade · Patchouli · KubeJS / datapacks (all recipes use `c:` and `astralvigil:` tags). Every balance
value lives in `astralvigil-server.toml`; worldgen is a datapack you can override. Built for modpacks such as
All the Mods 10 and 11.

## Requirements

NeoForge 21.1.266+ (Minecraft 1.21.1) or NeoForge 26.1.2.126+ (Minecraft 26.1.2), and **GeckoLib**.
